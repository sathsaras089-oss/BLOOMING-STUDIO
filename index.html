About Us

Our Story

Capturing the essence of love, light, and timeless elegance

Every Frame Tells a Story

At Studio Blooming, we believe that photography is more than just capturing images—it's about preserving emotions, freezing moments in time, and creating art that speaks to the soul.

Founded by Hasitha Udugampala, our studio has been dedicated to the craft of visual storytelling for over a decade. With an eye for detail and a passion for authenticity, we approach each session as a unique opportunity to create something extraordinary.

From the soft glow of golden hour to the intimate whispers between lovers, we find beauty in every moment. Our mission is simple: to give you photographs that you'll treasure for a lifetime.
